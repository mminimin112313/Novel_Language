# IU Awards & Recognition

IU is one of the most decorated artists in South Korean history.

## Major Music Awards
- **Melon Music Awards (MMA):** 13+ awards, including multiple Daesangs (Artist of the Year, Album of the Year).
- **Mnet Asian Music Awards (MAMA):** Multiple Best Female Artist and Best Vocal Performance awards.
- **Golden Disc Awards (GDA):** Numerous Digital Daesangs (Grand Prize).
- **Korean Music Awards (KMA):** Recognized for artistic merit and songwriting.
- **Gaon Chart Music Awards:** Record for most "Artist of the Year (Digital Music)" wins.

## Acting Awards
- **KBS Drama Awards (2013):** Best New Actress for *You Are the Best!*.
- **Baeksang Arts Awards:** Most Popular Actress (複数回). Nominated for Best Actress for *My Mister* and *Hotel del Luna*.
- **Chunsa Film Art Awards (2022):** Best New Actress for *Broker*.
- **Korean Association of Film Critics Awards (2022):** Best New Actress for *Broker*.

## State Honors
- **Presidential Commendation (2015):** For contribution to Korean popular culture and arts.
- **Prime Minister's Commendation (2011).**
- **Taxpayer Day Presidential Commendation (2020):** Exemplary taxpayer recognition.

## Records
- Most number-one songs on the Circle (formerly Gaon) Digital Chart.
- Most "Perfect All-Kills" (PAK) in Korean music chart history.
