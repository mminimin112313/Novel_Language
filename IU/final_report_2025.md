# IU Research Consolidated Summary (2025 Updated)

## Overview
IU (Lee Ji-eun) continues to dominate the South Korean entertainment industry as a multi-talented solo artist and actress. 2024 marked a major milestone with her first world tour and the release of her 6th EP.

## Key 2024-2025 Highlights

### Music & Performance
- **Album:** Released **"The Winning"** in February 2024. Double titles **"Shopper"** and **"Holssi"** topped charts, alongside the viral hit **"Love wins all"**.
- **World Tour:** Conducted the **2024 IU H.E.R. World Tour**, her first global scale tour visiting 18 cities including London, Berlin, Los Angeles, and Taipei.

### Acting (Filmography)
- **"When Life Gives You Tangerines" (폭싹 속았수다):** Completed filming for this high-budget Netflix series set in 1950s Jeju. Co-starring Park Bo-gum, expected to release in **2025**.

### Philanthropy
- Consistently donated 200M+ KRW for her birthday (May 16) and additional significant funds for Children's Day in 2024, maintaining her status as Korea's representative "Donation Angel."

## Conclusion
IU's expansion into the global market via her world tour and high-profile streaming series signals a new chapter in her 16-year career, transitioning from a domestic icon to a global superstar.
