# IU Philanthropy & Public Image

IU is widely known as the "Donation Angel" for her consistent and large-scale charitable contributions.

## Key Philanthropic Activities
- **Annual Donations:** Consistently donates 500 million+ KRW on her birthday and debut anniversaries.
- **Child Welfare:** Frequent supporter of ChildFund Korea (Green Umbrella Children's Foundation).
- **Education:** Established the "IU Scholarship" at Dongduk Girls' High School for students from low-income families.
- **Disaster Relief:** Significant contributions during COVID-19 and natural disasters (wildfires, floods).
- **Medical Support:** Donates to associations for the deaf, the elderly living alone, and cancer patients.
- **2024 Donations:** Contributed 200M KRW on her birthday and 100M KRW on Children's Day to help vulnerable children and families.


## Public Image
- **The Nation's Little Sister:** An early title gained through her wholesome and talented image.
- **The Nation's Sweetheart:** Reflecting her broad popularity across all age groups.
- **Professionalism:** Highly regarded for her dedication to music production and artistic integrity.
- **Fan Love:** Exceptionally close relationship with her fandom, UAENA, often preparing special lunch boxes or gifts.

## Cultural Impact
- Considered a "living legend" in the K-pop industry.
- A role model for many "Acting-dols" (idols who transition into acting).
- Her lyrics and music are praised for their relatability and poetic quality.
