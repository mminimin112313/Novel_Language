# IU Profile

- **Stage Name:** IU (아이유)
- **Birth Name:** Lee Ji-eun (이지은)

- **Birth Date:** May 16, 1993
- **Birthplace:** Seoul, South Korea
- **Agency:** EDAM Entertainment (formerly LOEN Entertainment / Kakao M)
- **Debut:** September 18, 2008
- **Fandom Name:** UAENA (유애나)
- **Nickname:** "The Nation's Little Sister," "The Nation's Sweetheart"

## Introduction
IU is one of the most successful and influential solo artists and actresses in South Korea. Her stage name "IU" is a combination of "I" and "You", symbolizing that "you and I become one through music."

## Key Achievements
- Most number-one songs on the Gaon Digital Chart.
- Highly respected "Acting-dol" with critically acclaimed roles.
- Known for her extensive philanthropy.
- **2024 H.E.R. World Tour:** Her first world tour covering 18 cities across Asia, Europe, and North America.
